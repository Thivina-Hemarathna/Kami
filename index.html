From my heart, with honesty  ·  No excuses, only truth

I am sorry.

I have thought long and hard about whether I even deserve to write these words — and I am still not sure I do. But I know that staying silent would be the greater wrong, so here I am, trying.

I am sorry. Not in the way people say it when they want something to be over — but in the way that keeps you up at night, in the way that makes you look back and wish, with everything you have, that you had been better. That is the kind of sorry this is.

I let you down. I know that. And I am not going to dress it up or explain it away, because you deserve more than excuses. You always deserved more than I gave you in those moments, and I see that clearly now.

What I did was not a reflection of how much you mean to me — if anything, losing you has shown me exactly how much that is. And it is more than I ever said out loud, more than I showed, more than you probably know.

I am not asking you to forget. I am not even asking you to forgive me right now — that is yours to give or keep, entirely on your own terms and in your own time. What I am asking is simply for you to know that I am truly, deeply sorry.

You are someone who deserved consistency, honesty, and care — every single day. I want to be the person who gives you that. I am working on being that person, not to win you back, but because knowing you has made me want to be better — full stop.

I'll always be there for you my pie. But I needed you to know that you were never, not for a single day, anything less than everything to me.
